Privacy Policy

Applies to the Khata Plus Android app (package in.khataplus.billing). Effective date: 30 October 2026.

In short

Stored online
Only your sign-in details (name, email, and an account ID), handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
Stored on your phone
All your business data: bills, parties, items, stock, payments, expenses and reports.
Not collected
No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. We never see your bills or your customers' details.
Your control
Delete your account anytime from Settings in the app.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google or with email and password, Firebase Authentication (a Google service) receives:

  • your name and email address,
  • your phone number, only if it is part of your Google account and Google shares it,
  • a unique account ID and the sign-in method you used.

We use this only to let you sign in to the app and keep your account secure.

2. Your business data stays on your device

Everything you enter in Khata Plus, including your firm profile, GSTIN, customers and suppliers, their phone numbers and balances, invoices, items, stock, payments and expenses, is saved in a database inside the app on your phone. It is not uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

Because this data lives only on your phone, uninstalling the app or clearing its data will delete it. Use the backup option in the app to keep a copy.

3. Backups and sharing

When you create a backup, export a CSV, save an invoice as PDF, or share an invoice on WhatsApp or another app, the file goes only where you choose to send or save it. Data you share with another app is then handled under that app's own privacy policy.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed for signing in.
  • Files (only when you pick them): to add your business logo or signature, or to restore a backup.

5. Third-party services

Khata Plus uses Google Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In to manage sign-in. Google processes your sign-in details under the Google Privacy Policy. The app does not contain advertising or analytics tools.

6. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information or your business data with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

7. Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your sign-in details (name and email) from Firebase. Business data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. You can also email us to request deletion.

8. Security

Sign-in is protected by Google Firebase. Your business data is protected by your phone's own security, so keep your phone locked and your backups in a safe place.

9. Children

Khata Plus is a business tool meant for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
